# Research Sources

The URLs below were reviewed during this pass. For game pages, the notes distinguish documented features from design interpretation. GDC Vault entries were reviewed through their public session overviews; this pass does not claim to have watched restricted full videos.

## Games And Primary Materials

### A1. Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes - Official Game Page

URL: https://keeptalkinggame.com/

Used for: the defuser/experts split, communication-first premise, procedural puzzle and mission framing, and official screenshots.

### A2. Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes - Remote Play Guide

URL: https://keeptalkinggame.com/how-to-play-remotely/

Used for: one defuser, multiple experts, voice as the connection, rounds of five minutes or less, and the developer's recommended 2-4 player group.

### A3. Keep Talking - Bomb Defusal Manual

URL: http://www.bombmanual.com/

Used for: studying how complex rules are externalized into a readable manual that a different role can interpret.

### A4. Operation: Tango - Steam Page

URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1335790/Operation_Tango/

Used for: Agent/Hacker role separation, voice-only connection, co-op puzzles that cannot be completed alone, and the explicit focus on communication rather than difficulty.

### A5. Blind Drive - Official Game Page

URL: https://www.blinddrivegame.com/

Used for: audio-first first-person play, two-button input, voice acting, haptics, text-to-speech support, and the principle that audio can be the primary game space.

### A6. A Blind Legend - Developer/Production Page

URL: https://www.dowino.com/realisations/serious-game-ablindlegend/

Used for: 3D/binaural sound as navigation, audio landmarks, and the accessibility-oriented goal of making an adventure playable through hearing.

### A7. Phasmophobia - Steam Page

URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/739630/Phasmophobia/

Used for: four-player investigation, evidence collection, unique ghost traits, maps with hiding spots and layouts, voice recognition, and support from a truck via CCTV and sensors.

### A8. Lethal Company - Steam Page

URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1966720/Lethal_Company/

Used for: four-player crew scale, quota pressure, abandoned industrial spaces, ship-based radar/terminal support, extraction risk, and developer commentary about replayability and feedback.

### A9. Among Us - Official Innersloth Page

URL: https://www.innersloth.com/games/among-us/

Used for: teamwork/betrayal framing, 4-15 player scale, task/camera/meeting structure, official role explanations, gameplay screenshots, and the Guardian Angel post-death role reference.

### A10. Dead by Daylight - Official Game Page

URL: https://deadbydaylight.com/game/

Used for: one Killer versus four Survivors, role-specific goals, hide-and-seek pressure, character presentation, and strongly differentiated map identities.

### A11. Hades - Official Supergiant Page

URL: https://www.supergiantgames.com/games/hades/

Used for: official gameplay screenshots, cast presentation, color/shape studies, animation references, and the idea that a small stylized game can have a distinct character language without realistic assets.

### A12. VALORANT - Official Agent Roster

URL: https://playvalorant.com/en-us/agents/

Used for: a primary-source roster study of named, role-readable characters and the importance of differentiating silhouettes and ability identities.

### A13. Cyberpunk Universe - Official CD PROJEKT RED Page

URL: https://www.cyberpunk.net/us/en/

Used only as a high-level reference for urban technology, high contrast, neon as an accent, and the separation between the Cyberpunk 2077 games and Edgerunners anime. No Cyberpunk assets or exact UI are to be copied.

## Research Limits

- No external assets, characters, maps, logos, screenshots, or code from these references should be copied into Blind Run.
- Several GDC pages expose public session overviews but not the full talk. The documents use only the claims present in those overviews.
- Game pages describe intended features; they are not independent proof that every feature is balanced or successful.
- Repository conclusions are based on the current checkout and should be revalidated after any multiplayer or asset changes.
